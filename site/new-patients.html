New patients

Preparing for your visit

Dr. Verma's office is opening soon. Join the waitlist now and we'll call you to schedule as soon as appointments open.

Join the Waitlist

What to bring

  • Photo ID and insurance card(s)
  • A referral, if your plan requires one
  • A list of your medications
  • Your glasses and contact lenses
  • MRI or CT reports and images (on disc or a portal link) and recent eye exam records
  • For Workers' Compensation visits: your WCB claim number and the insurance carrier's name and address

How long it takes

Plan for 1.5 to 2 hours for a first visit, since testing is often done the same day. Your pupils may be dilated, so bring sunglasses and consider having someone drive you home.

If you were referred

Ask your doctor's office to send your records before your visit so Dr. Verma can review them in advance.

Insurance

Insurance details are coming soon. We will post the plans we participate in before the office opens. If we are out of network with your plan, we will give you a cost estimate before your visit.

Workers' Compensation

We accept New York State Workers' Compensation for work-related eye and vision injuries. Please bring your WCB claim number to your first visit.

Telehealth

Some follow-up visits can be done by secure video. We will let you know if your visit qualifies.

Uninsured or self-pay patients

You have the right to receive a Good Faith Estimate of expected charges. Ask us for one when you schedule. Learn more.
