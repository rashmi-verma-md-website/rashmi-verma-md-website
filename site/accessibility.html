Accessibility and Language Help

We want every patient to be able to reach us, understand their care, and visit comfortably.

At our office

Free aids and services are available, including large-print materials and qualified sign language interpreters. If you need an accommodation for your visit, please tell us when you are contacted to schedule, or email info@neuroophtho.com.

Service animals welcome. Pets and emotional support animals are not permitted.

This website

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. If you have trouble using any part of this site, email info@neuroophtho.com and we will help you get the information another way.
