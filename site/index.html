Neuro-ophthalmology is a subspecialty that cares for diseases affecting both the eyes and the brain, and the connections between them. When a visit to the neurologist or the ophthalmologist hasn't given you answers, it is time to see a neuro-ophthalmologist.

Conditions treated

Understanding your diagnosis

Neuro-ophthalmic conditions can be complex and worrying. Dr. Verma takes time to explain your diagnosis in plain language and guide you through your care.

Optic neuritis

Inflammation of the optic nerve that causes sudden vision loss or pain with eye movement, often associated with multiple sclerosis or other autoimmune conditions.

Papilledema and intracranial pressure

Swelling of the optic nerve head from raised pressure inside the skull, which can threaten vision if not diagnosed and managed promptly.

Double vision (diplopia)

Seeing two images of one object, from nerve palsies, muscle disorders, or neurological conditions affecting eye alignment.

Visual field loss

Loss of side or central vision that can signal a problem along the visual pathway from the eye to the brain, including stroke or tumors.
