Privacy Policy

Website privacy

Rashmi Verma, MD respects your privacy. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you contact us or request an appointment, we collect only the information you choose to send, such as your name, phone number, and email address, and use it only to respond to you and arrange your care. We do not sell your information.

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Our appointment request form is kept in a secure system covered by HIPAA safeguards. Regular email is less secure, so please use the form or call us rather than emailing detailed medical information.

Your health information

How we use and protect your health information, and your rights, are described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.
