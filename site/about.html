About Dr. Verma

Meet Rashmi Verma, MD

Dr. Verma is a neurology-trained neuro-ophthalmologist who cares for adults and children with disorders of the optic nerve, the brain, and the connections between the eye and the brain.

She has had a distinguished academic career, with dual fellowship training in neuro-ophthalmology and pediatric ophthalmology at the University of Michigan and Akron Children's Hospital, and neurology residency at Mount Sinai. She is committed to dedicated, compassionate care for every patient.

A subspecialist you can talk to

Dr. Verma completed fellowship training in neuro-ophthalmology at the W.K. Kellogg Eye Center, University of Michigan, and in pediatric ophthalmology and adult strabismus at Akron Children's Hospital. She completed her neurology residency at Mount Sinai Beth Israel / Mount Sinai West in New York and is board certified in neurology by the American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology.

She has held faculty appointments, each with joint roles across ophthalmology, neurology, and pediatrics, at:

  • Northwell Health, Donald and Barbara Zucker School of Medicine at Hofstra/Northwell
  • Saint Louis University School of Medicine
  • Montefiore Medical Center, Albert Einstein College of Medicine
  • Stony Brook University, Renaissance School of Medicine (Director of Neuro-Ophthalmology, 2026)

She has trained both ophthalmology and neurology residents and taught medical students throughout her career, and has given more than 50 invited lectures, Grand Rounds, and presentations at national and international meetings. She is a Fellow of the North American Neuro-Ophthalmology Society (NANOS), serves on the abstract committee for the journal Neuro-Ophthalmology, and reviews for the Journal of Neuro-Ophthalmology and the Oman Journal of Ophthalmology.

Areas of expertise

Idiopathic intracranial hypertension (IIH)

A special interest in the medical and surgical management of IIH in adults and children, including papilledema evaluation, weight management, medication, and surgery when indicated.

Pediatric neuro-ophthalmology

Fellowship-trained care for children with optic nerve abnormalities, visual field defects, nystagmus, strabismus, and other conditions, with a family-centered approach.

Cranial nerve palsies and myasthenia gravis

Diagnosis and management of double vision, cranial nerve palsies, and ocular myasthenia gravis.

Vision loss after stroke

Assessment and rehabilitation planning for visual field loss after stroke or other events affecting the visual pathway.
